Settings

Appearance

Theme
Pick a colour to change, then adjust it. Changed colours are listed above; click one to edit it again, or its × to drop it.
Window
0% is solid. Higher lets the desktop show through the window, blurred or clear.
Tree icons
One colour per level of the tree, top level first, repeating deeper down.

Editor

Writing
Misspelled words get a red underline; right-click one for suggestions.
Typing **bold**, *italic*, `code`, ~~strike~~ or ``` formats as you go, pasted plain text is read as Markdown, and labels like [GOAL] are tinted. On by default; turn it off for notes full of shell commands and regexes. Lists (- , 1. ) always work. Also in the Format menu.
Off: text typed after coloured or highlighted text, or on the next line, is back to normal. Typing inside a coloured word keeps its colour, and a colour picked with nothing selected applies to what you type next until the caret moves. On: the colour continues until you change it.
Images
Embedded images keep a note self-contained. Files keep notes small and readable in other editors.

Keyboard

Off turns every shortcut below off at once. The standard macOS keys (⌘C, ⌘V, ⌘Z, ⌘A, ⌘Q, ⌘W) keep working. To drop a single shortcut, click it and press Backspace.
Click a shortcut and press the new keys. Backspace clears it, Esc keeps the old one. A combination used twice is marked in red.
Changes preview live in the notebook window. ↩ saves, Esc cancels.
